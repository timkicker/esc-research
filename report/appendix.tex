\section*{Appendix: features and settings}

\small
\textbf{Panel.}
One row per voter, receiver, year and vote type (jury or public) in the final, 19\,800 rows.
A pair that gave nothing keeps zero points, because only the top ten score and dropping the zeros would leave a panel of successes.
Self votes are dropped.
The pooled rest-of-the-world public vote is a voter without geography and is left out of the pair statistics.

\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
Feature & Meaning\\
\midrule
\texttt{log\_dist} & log of the distance between the two countries (CEPII)\\
\texttt{contig}, \texttt{smctry} & shared border, same country (CEPII)\\
\texttt{comlang\_off}, \texttt{comlang\_ethno} & shared official or ethnic language (CEPII)\\
\texttt{colony}, \texttt{comcol} & colonial tie, common colonizer (CEPII)\\
\texttt{past\_points}, \texttt{past\_meetings} & mean points the voter gave the receiver in earlier finals, number of earlier finals\\
\texttt{web\_interest}, \texttt{youtube\_interest} & Google Trends interest for the entry in the voting country, cut the day before the final\\
\texttt{running\_position} & running order scaled to $[0,1]$\\
lyrics & sentiment, theme, repetition in verse and chorus (automatic analysis)\\
\texttt{receiver\_effect} & mean of what all other voters gave the same entry, explanatory only\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\medskip
\textbf{Feature sets.}
\emph{pair} uses the first nine features.
The others add the lyrics, the running order, the two Trends series or both.
Sets containing the receiver effect are labelled explanatory and never reported as forecasts.

\textbf{Models.}
Ridge regression with the penalty chosen from 13 values between $10^{-3}$ and $10^{3}$ by the built-in leave-one-out cross-validation on the training rows.
Gradient boosting (histogram based) with depth 4, learning rate 0.06 and 300 iterations. The decomposition uses a minimum leaf size of 200.
Random forest with 200 trees, minimum leaf size 20 and half of the features per split.
Missing values are filled with the training-year median.
All seeds are fixed (20260501 for the baseline, 0 for the models).

\textbf{Validation.}
Each year is held out whole.
The model is fitted on the pair rows of the other nine finals and predicts the pair rows of the held-out final.
The predicted points are summed per entry and turned into places that are compared with the actual places of the final.
The rank error is the mean absolute difference between the predicted and the actual place, averaged over finals.
The chance baseline shuffles the actual places within each final 1\,000 times; its mean is 8.5 for the jury and 8.4 for the public.
