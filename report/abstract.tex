Since 2016 every country in the Eurovision final has given two separate sets of points, one from a national jury and one from the public. We used this to measure, for juries and public apart, how much of the points one country gives another comes from the entry and how much from the relationship between the two countries. On finals the models had not seen, the entry alone explains 24.9\,\% of the variance in the jury points and 40.3\,\% in the public points. The relationship alone explains 2.8\,\% for the juries and 12.5\,\% for the public, about four and a half times as much. A forecast from country pair data and Google Trends places entries 2.1 (juries) and 2.9 (public) places closer to their real rank than chance.
