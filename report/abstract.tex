We predict how many points every country gives every other country in the Eurovision final, using only what is known the day before: earlier votes between the two countries, Google searches for the entry in the voting country, distance, language and the running order. Summed per entry, the predictions give a scoreboard. Gradient boosting, trained on 2008 to 2019 and chosen on where it put the winners of 2021 to 2023, puts the real winner of the test finals 2024, 2025 and 2026 in first, third and third place. When each of the 18 finals is left out once, the winner lands in its top three in 9 of them and on average at place 5.7, where a random order gives 13.3. Three test finals are few and we settled on this measure only after seeing first results. Below the top the scoreboard stays rough at 6.42 places off against 8.43 for a random order. On the validation finals the searches did most to find the winner, while the lyrics and the age and sex of the artist barely mattered.
