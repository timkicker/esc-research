We predict the points every country gives every other country in the Eurovision final, using only what is known the day before: earlier votes between the two countries, Google searches for the entry in the voting country, distance, language and the running order. Summed per entry, the predictions give a scoreboard. Trained on 2008 to 2019 and chosen on where it put the winners of 2021 to 2023, gradient boosting puts the real winner of the test finals 2024, 2025 and 2026 in first, third and third place. Leaving out each of the 18 finals once, the winner lands in its top three nine times, against the 13th place a random order gives on average. Three test finals are few and the measure was settled after the first results. The rest of the scoreboard stays rough at 6.42 places off against 8.43 for a random order. Searches and earlier votes find the winner, while the lyrics and the artist's age and sex do not help.
