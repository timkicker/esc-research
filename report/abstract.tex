We predict the points every country gives every other country in the Eurovision final, using only what is known the day before: earlier votes between the two countries, Google searches for the entry in the voting country, distance, language and the running order. Summed per entry, the predictions give a scoreboard. Trained on 2008 to 2019 and chosen on 2021 to 2023, gradient boosting puts the real winner of the test finals 2024, 2025 and 2026 in first, second and fourth place. With three test finals and a measure we settled on after the first results, this is a hint and not proof. The whole scoreboard stays rough: 6.29 places off on average against 8.43 for a random order. Simple linear models do slightly better there. Earlier votes and searches carry the prediction, while the lyrics and the age and sex of the artist do not help.
